
# Returns and Refund Policy

## Return Eligibility

Customers can request a return within 30 calendar days of receiving their order.

Products must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.

## Non-Returnable Products

Personalized products and digital products are not eligible for returns.

## Refund Processing

After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Damaged Products

Customers who receive damaged products should contact customer support and provide their order ID and photographs of the damage.

The support team will review the request before deciding the appropriate resolution.
