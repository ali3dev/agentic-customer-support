
# Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after order dispatch.

## Express Shipping

Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days after order dispatch.

## Order Tracking

Customers can request their order status using their unique order ID.

An estimated delivery date is provided when available.

## Delivery Delays

Unexpected weather, carrier disruptions, and operational issues may delay delivery.

Customers should contact support if their order has not arrived after the estimated delivery date.
