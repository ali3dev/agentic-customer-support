
# General Customer Support FAQs

## How do I return an item?

Contact customer support with your order ID. The support team will review the return eligibility and guide you through the process.

## When will I receive my refund?

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product is received and inspected.

## Can I return a personalized product?

No. Personalized products are not eligible for returns under the current return policy.

## How can I track my order?

Provide your unique order ID to customer support to check the current order status and available delivery estimate.
